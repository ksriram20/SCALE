# Music bed credits & licensing

These ambient tracks play *under* the narration (see `tts.music` in config.yaml),
low-passed and ducked so they sit soft and warm behind the voice. Drop your own
CC0 / royalty-free tracks here to add to the rotation.

## Currently included

- **Scott Buckley — "Sleep"** (`ScottBuckley-Sleep.mp3`)
  - License: **CC BY 4.0** — free to use (incl. commercial) **with attribution**.
  - Attribution: *Music by Scott Buckley – www.scottbuckley.com.au*
  - Source: Internet Archive — https://archive.org/details/scott-buckley-sleep

- **Cosmic Dharma — "Magic Forest", "Zen Garden"** (`ZenGarden-*.mp3`)
  - License: **CC BY-NC 2.0** — free for **non-commercial** use with attribution.
  - Source: Internet Archive — https://archive.org/details/cosmic_dharma_magic_forest_zen_garden

## Notes

- For *personal* commute listening, all of the above are fine.
- If you ever **publish/monetize** episodes, drop the BY-NC tracks and prefer
  **CC0** (no attribution) — e.g. John Bartmann's *Straylight Drones* (CC0):
  https://freemusicarchive.org/music/John_Bartmann/100-ambient-atmospheric-soundtracks-straylight-drones-collection
- The bed is low-passed (`tts.music.lowpass_hz`) and ducked, so even a busier
  track sits soft behind the narration.
